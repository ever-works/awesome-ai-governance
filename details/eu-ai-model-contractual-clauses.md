## Overview

The EU AI Model Contractual Clauses are standardized contract language from the European Commission for procuring AI systems in alignment with the EU AI Act. They provide public buyers with model clauses to incorporate AI Act requirements into procurement contracts.

## Features

- Model contractual clauses for AI procurement
- Aligned with EU AI Act requirements
- Developed by the European Commission
- Free resource for public buyers
- Standardized contract language
