## Overview

Regula is an open-source CLI tool for EU AI Act risk scanning, conformity evidence pack generation, and CrowS-Pairs bias evaluation.

## Features

- EU AI Act risk scanning
- Conformity evidence packs
- CrowS-Pairs bias evaluation
- Command-line interface
- Compliance automation

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
