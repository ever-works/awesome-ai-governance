## Overview

UNE 0086:2025 is a Spanish standard on Measurement of energy consumption, carbon footprint, water consumption and performance of Artificial Intelligence systems, published by UNE.

## Status

Published

## Scope

Medición del consumo energético, huella de carbono, consumo del agua y rendimiento de sistemas de Inteligencia Artificial.

## Availability

Available through the UNE standards portal.
