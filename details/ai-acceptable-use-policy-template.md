## Overview

The AI Acceptable Use Policy Template from NexoDocs is a free editable Word document that helps organizations establish policies for staff use of generative AI, supporting compliance with Article 4 (AI literacy) of the EU AI Act.

## Features

- Editable Word document template
- Focus on generative AI acceptable use
- Supports Article 4 AI literacy obligations
- Free download
- Staff-facing policy guidance

## Pricing

Free.

## Deployment

Downloadable file (Word).
