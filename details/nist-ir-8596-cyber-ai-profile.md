## Overview

NIST IR 8596 (Cyber AI Profile) is a NIST profile crossing the Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 against AI in three focus areas: Secure AI System Components, Defend with AI, and Thwart AI-Enabled Threats. It operates across six CSF functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover. Preliminary Draft, December 2025.
