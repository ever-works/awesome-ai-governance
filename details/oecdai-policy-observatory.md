## Overview

OECD.AI Policy Observatory is an interactive platform from the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD). It hosts a database of over 900 national AI policies and provides tools and resources for building trustworthy AI systems based on the OECD AI Principles.

## Features

- Database of 900+ national AI policies and initiatives
- OECD AI Principles tracking and adherence
- Country-level AI policy comparisons
- AI policy tools and guidance resources
- Live data on global AI governance developments
