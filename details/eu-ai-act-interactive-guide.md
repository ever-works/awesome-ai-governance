## Overview

EU AI Act Interactive Guide is an open-source comprehensive interactive guide for startups and SMEs, featuring a compliance checker and risk assessment tools for EU AI Act compliance.

## Features

- Interactive compliance guide
- Startup and SME focused
- Compliance checker
- Risk assessment
- EU AI Act compliance support

## Pricing
