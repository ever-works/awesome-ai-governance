## Overview

ETSI EN 304 223 V2.1.1 (2025-12) is a European standard for Securing Artificial Intelligence - Baseline Cyber Security Requirements for AI Models and Systems, published by ETSI.

## Status

Published

## Scope

Securing Artificial Intelligence (SAI); Baseline Cyber Security Requirements for AI Models and Systems.

## Availability

Available as PDF from the ETSI deliverables portal.
