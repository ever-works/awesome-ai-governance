## Overview

GOPAL is an Apache-2.0 library of Rego policies for AI-governance regulations.

## Features

- 85 Rego policies
- Encodes EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ICAO/FAA/EASA aviation, FERPA/COPPA, fair lending
- Executable allow/deny checks for OPA engine
- Versioned per framework
- Allow/deny tests in CI
- Apache-2.0 licensed

## Deployment

Self-hosted (OPA engine)
