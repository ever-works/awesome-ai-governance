## Overview

MCP EU AI Act Scanner is an open-source scanner that analyzes codebases for EU AI Act and GDPR compliance gaps, generating auditor-ready Annex IV evidence packages.

## Features

- Codebase scanning for compliance gaps
- EU AI Act compliance checking
- GDPR compliance checking
- Annex IV evidence package generation
- Auditor-ready documentation

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
