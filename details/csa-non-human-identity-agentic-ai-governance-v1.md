## Overview

The CSA Non-Human Identity & Agentic AI Governance v1 is a Cloud Security Alliance whitepaper recommending a 6-field NHI registry (Identity, Owning Team, Business Purpose, Systems Accessed, Privilege Scope, Expiration/Review Date) for managing non-human and agentic identities. Published May 2026.
