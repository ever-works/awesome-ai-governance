## Overview

capsule-anchor is software for running a vendor-neutral SCITT Transparency Service (RFC 9162).

## Features

- Vendor-neutral SCITT Transparency Service
- Submit a digest, get a COSE receipt
- Append-only, independently auditable log
- Offline verification
- Self-hosted or free community instance
