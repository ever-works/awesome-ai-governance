## Overview

Regulus is an EU & UK compliance plane for Google ADK.

## Features

- 10 regulations encoded: EU AI Act, GDPR, DORA, NIS2, EHDS, UK GDPR, FCA SYSC, PRA SS1/23, PRA SS2/21, NHS DSPT
- 6 governance frameworks as runtime ADK BasePlugin profiles
- Hash-chained audit envelopes
- GRC adapters: ServiceNow IRM, OneTrust, MetricStream

## Deployment

Self-hosted
