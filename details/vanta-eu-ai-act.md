## Overview

Vanta EU AI Act is a compliance automation module within the Vanta trust management platform, focused on EU AI Act compliance automation with risk classification and continuous monitoring.

## Features

- Risk classification
- Evidence reuse from NIST AI RMF
- Evidence reuse from ISO 42001
- Continuous monitoring
- Compliance automation
