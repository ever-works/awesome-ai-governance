## Overview

The IEEE 7000 Series Standards are a collection of IEEE standards addressing ethical considerations in AI system design and deployment. The series includes standards for transparency, data privacy, algorithmic bias, and wellbeing metrics, providing a framework for ethically aligned AI development.

## Features

- P7001: Transparency of Autonomous Systems
- P7002: Data Privacy Process
- P7003: Algorithmic Bias Considerations
- P7010: Wellbeing Metrics
- Ethically aligned design framework
- IEEE standards development process
