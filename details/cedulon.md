## Overview

Cedulon is an audit layer for agent-to-agent commerce that provides signed trade manifests, default-deny spend policy, COSE_Sign1 receipts, epoch checkpoints, and completeness checks.

## Features

- Signed trade manifests
- Default-deny spend policy
- COSE_Sign1 receipts
- Epoch checkpoints
- Completeness check of receipts against a rail extract
- MCP server settles on mock rail
- Not a payment rail
- Specified in IETF Internet-Drafts
