## Overview

Vaara is an open-source Python runtime evidence layer for AI agents that provides policy-gated tool calls, hash-chained tamper-evident audit trails with external time anchoring, and independently verifiable execution receipts, aligned with EU AI Act Article 14 (human oversight) and Article 12 (record-keeping).

## Features

- Policy-gated tool calls
- Hash-chained tamper-evident audit trails
- External time anchoring
- Independently verifiable execution receipts
- EU AI Act Article 14 alignment
- EU AI Act Article 12 alignment
- Python runtime
