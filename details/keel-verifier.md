## Overview

keel-verifier is an open-source (MIT) CLI that checks signed AI Permit records offline against Keel's published keys.

## Features

- Checks signed AI Permit records (decisions, reviews and closures)
- Offline verification
- Uses Keel's published keys
- Does not contact Keel during verification
- MIT licensed
