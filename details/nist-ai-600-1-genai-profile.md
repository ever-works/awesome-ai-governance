## Overview

NIST AI 600-1 is a NIST profile of the AI Risk Management Framework focused on generative AI. It enumerates 12 risk categories: CBRN, Confabulation, Dangerous Content, Data Privacy, Environmental Impacts, Harmful Bias, Human-AI Configuration, Information Integrity, Information Security, Intellectual Property, Obscene Content, and Value Chain. It provides 200+ suggested actions across the Govern/Map/Measure/Manage functions. Published July 2024.
