## Overview

PRML / falsify is a Pre-Registered ML Manifest specification (CC BY 4.0) that commits an evaluation claim (metric, comparator, threshold, dataset hash, seed, producer identity) to a SHA-256 hash before the experiment runs.

## Features

- Pre-registered ML manifest specification
- Tamper-evident audit trail
- Subcategory crosswalks for EU AI Act Article 12
- Subcategory crosswalks for NIST AI RMF
- Subcategory crosswalks for ISO/IEC 42001
- Four byte-equivalent reference implementations across 21 conformance vectors
