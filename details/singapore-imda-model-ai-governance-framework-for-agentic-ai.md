## Overview

The Singapore IMDA Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI defines eight components of an agentic system: Model, Instructions, Memory, Planning & Reasoning, Tools, Protocols, Controls, and Logging/Monitoring. It also defines four governance dimensions: bounding risks, human accountability, technical controls, and end-user responsibility. v1.5 published 20 May 2026.
