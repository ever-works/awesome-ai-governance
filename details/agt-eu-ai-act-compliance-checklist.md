## Overview

AGT EU AI Act Compliance Checklist maps Agent Governance Toolkit controls to EU AI Act requirements, supporting compliance mapping and validation for AI agent governance.

## Features

- Control-to-requirement mapping
- EU AI Act compliance checklist
- Agent Governance Toolkit integration
- Compliance validation
