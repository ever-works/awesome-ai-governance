## Overview

The EU AI Act Risk Classification Decision Record is a free editable XLSX template from Brandquill for documenting risk classification evidence under Articles 5, 6, and Annex III of the EU AI Act.

## Features

- Editable XLSX template
- Covers Article 5 (prohibited practices) classification
- Covers Article 6 (high-risk AI systems) classification
- Covers Annex III (high-risk use cases) classification
- Free download

## Pricing

Free.

## Deployment

Downloadable file (XLSX).
