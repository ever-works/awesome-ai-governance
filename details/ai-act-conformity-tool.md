## Overview

The AI Act Conformity Tool is an interactive web tool from the European DIGITAL SME Alliance designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises assess the risk level of their AI systems and obtain a conformity report aligned with the EU AI Act.

## Features

- Interactive risk level assessment for AI systems
- Conformity report generation
- Tailored for SMEs
- EU AI Act compliance guidance
- Web-based tool, no installation required

## Pricing

Free to use.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (web application).
