## Overview

PALO Framework is an open-source local-first platform providing EU AI Act risk tiering, Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA), lifecycle controls, and evidence workflows.

## Features

- EU AI Act risk tiering
- FRIA (Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment)
- Lifecycle controls
- Evidence workflows
- Local-first deployment

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
