## Overview

CoSAI Risk Map v1 is the Coalition for Secure AI's component-level risk taxonomy for agentic AI systems. It defines 23 components, 35 controls, 36 risks (including 6 agentic-specific), 10 personas, and 8 lifecycle stages. It cross-walks to MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI RMF, STRIDE, OWASP LLM Top 10, ISO 22989, and EU AI Act. Launched June 2026.
