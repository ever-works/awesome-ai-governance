## Overview

CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 is the official Joint Technical Committee responsible for developing European harmonized standards supporting the EU AI Act. It develops standards on AI trustworthiness, risk management, and conformity assessment that provide presumption of conformity with AI Act requirements.

## Features

- Development of harmonized European AI standards
- Support for EU AI Act conformity and presumption of conformity
- Focus on AI trustworthiness and risk management
- Conformity assessment standards
- Official European standardization body (JTC 21)
