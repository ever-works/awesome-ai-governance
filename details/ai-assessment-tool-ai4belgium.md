## Overview

AI Assessment Tool (AI4Belgium) is an open-source interactive tool based on ALTAI (Assessment List for Trustworthy AI) recommendations for assessing AI trustworthiness.

## Features

- AI trustworthiness assessment
- ALTAI-based recommendations
- Interactive questionnaire
- EU AI Act compliance preparation

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
