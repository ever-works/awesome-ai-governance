## Overview

AI FactSheets is a methodology from IBM Research for creating standardized documentation of AI models and services. FactSheets provide information about a model's purpose, performance, safety, security, and provenance to support transparency and accountability in AI systems.

## Features

- Structured methodology for AI model documentation
- Covers purpose, performance, safety, security, and provenance
- Human-centered design approach
- Supports AI governance and compliance workflows
- Applicable across different model types and deployment contexts

## Notes

This is a research methodology and publication rather than a software tool. Refer to the IBM Research publication for implementation details.
