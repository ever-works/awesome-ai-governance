## Overview

Retrace is an open-source scanner that reports AI chatbots and AI-notice wording on a website, supporting EU AI Act Article 50 evidence collection for transparency obligations.

## Features

- AI chatbot detection on websites
- AI-notice wording reporting
- Article 50 evidence collection
- Open-source scanner

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
