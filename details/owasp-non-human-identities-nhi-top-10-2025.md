## Overview

The OWASP Non-Human Identities (NHI) Top 10 (2025) is an OWASP awareness list of the top 10 risks for non-human identities, including Improper Offboarding, Secret Leakage, Vulnerable Third-Party NHI, Insecure Authentication, Overprivileged NHI, Insecure Cloud Deployment Configurations, Long-Lived Secrets, Environment Isolation, NHI Reuse, and Human Use of NHI. Risk-ranked using the OWASP Risk Rating Methodology. Published December 2024.
