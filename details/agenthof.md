## Overview

Agenthof is a config-driven control plane for governing external agents.

## Features

- Governs at model, tool/MCP, and exec boundaries
- Per-call credential injection
- Per-agent least-privilege enforcement
- Hash-chained, tamper-evident audit ledger
- Apache-2.0 licensed
- Written in Go

## Deployment

Self-hosted
