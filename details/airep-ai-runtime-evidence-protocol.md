## Overview

AIREP (AI Runtime Evidence Protocol) is an experimental, vendor-neutral runtime-evidence format for AI decisions, control delivery, execution, and observed effects. It uses signed/hash-linked artifacts and supports explicit missing or unevaluated states.

## Category

Frameworks & Standards

## Pricing

Open specification, free to use. Not a ratified standard.
