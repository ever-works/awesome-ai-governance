## Overview

DARR consolidates canonical AI risks for organizations that deploy AI systems, crosswalked to ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act, and MITRE ATLAS.

## Features

- Canonical AI risks for deployers
- Crosswalk to ISO/IEC 42001
- Crosswalk to EU AI Act
- Crosswalk to MITRE ATLAS
- Open data (CC BY 4.0)
