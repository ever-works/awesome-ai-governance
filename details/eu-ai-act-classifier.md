## Overview

EU AI Act Classifier is an open-source deterministic risk-tier classifier that provides pinpoint citations, obligation tracking, and review gates for EU AI Act compliance.

## Features

- Deterministic risk-tier classification
- Pinpoint article citations
- Obligation tracking
- Review gates
- EU AI Act compliance support

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
