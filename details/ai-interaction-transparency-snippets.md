## Overview

AI Interaction Transparency Snippets is an open-source project providing source-traced examples for AI-chat and synthetic-content notices to support EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations.

## Features

- Source-traced examples
- AI-chat transparency notices
- Synthetic-content notices
- Article 50 compliance support

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
