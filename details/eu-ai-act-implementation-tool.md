## Overview

AI Act Implementation Tool (Algorithm Audit) is an open-source tool that performs risk classification of algorithmic systems using simplified questionnaires for EU AI Act compliance.

## Features

- Risk classification of algorithmic systems
- Simplified questionnaires
- EU AI Act compliance assessment
- Open-source

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
