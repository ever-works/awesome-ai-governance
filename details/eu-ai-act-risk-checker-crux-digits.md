## Overview

EU AI Act Risk Checker (Crux Digits) is an open-source browser-based risk-tier triage tool with per-article citations, updated for the 2026 Digital Omnibus.

## Features

- Browser-based risk-tier triage
- Per-article citations
- Updated for 2026 Digital Omnibus
- EU AI Act compliance assessment

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
