## Overview

Oathline binds a human's yes to exactly what they were shown or heard, executes the approved agent action once, and records it in a hash chain that detects edits.

## Features

- Binds human approval to exactly what was shown or heard
- Executes approved agent action once
- Hash chain that detects edits
- audit.head() anchored outside the log for detecting privileged rewrites
- Python, no dependencies
